# Coursework 02: AI-Assisted Research Paper on an Agentic AI Workflow

## Objective

Develop the agentic AI workflow proposed in Coursework 01 into a research paper. Explain the real-world problem, the proposed workflow, the agent design, and the evidence for why the approach could be valuable.

Use the workplace, office, internship, volunteer, family-business, student-organization, or other real setting selected for Coursework 01. If needed, refine the original idea as you research it. Do not include confidential, personal, or proprietary information.

Write the paper in LaTeX using [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/) if possible, then submit the compiled paper as a PDF.

## Use AI throughout the task

Use AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or similar tools to explore the topic, locate relevant research, improve the analysis, critique drafts, develop figures, and edit the paper. Use an AI coding tool such as Claude Code, Codex, or a similar assistant if you build or improve a prototype.

You remain responsible for the submitted work. Check AI-generated claims against reliable sources, review citations, correct errors, and make the final arguments your own. Include a short **AI-use disclosure** stating which tools were used and how they contributed.

## What the paper should communicate

The paper should clearly communicate:

- The real-world setting, problem, and current manual process.
- Why the problem is important and who is affected.
- The proposed agentic AI workflow and how it changes the process.
- The purpose, inputs, outputs, tools, prompts, decision rules, and handoffs of every agent.
- The points at which people review, approve, or intervene.
- The data, systems, or external tools the workflow would need.
- Expected value, such as time saved, improved quality, consistency, or better decision support.
- Limitations, risks, privacy considerations, and potential failure cases.
- How the workflow could be evaluated, including practical success measures.
- A workflow diagram and any other figures that make the design easy to understand.
- A link or citation to any source code used to implement the workflow.
